# Case: Meridian Insurance

Proposed system: reads a submitted claim and flags it for extra review (flag / no flag) with a short reason.
Users: claims handlers. Affected people: policyholders making claims.
Business goal: route the ~5,000 weekly claims so handlers look at the riskier ones first.
Makers' claim: "It only assists. A human always makes the final decision; the fraud-review flag is advisory."
Note: this brief refers to the organisation's coverage exclusions policy, which should be in documents/.
